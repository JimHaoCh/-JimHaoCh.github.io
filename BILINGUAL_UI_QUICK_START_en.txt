NE-501 v1.4.1 quick start

Choose the mode above the step bar.
Manual Control: 1 Connect & map > 2 Syringes > 3 Manual priming > 4 Manual settings > 5 Review & start.
Timeline simulation: 1 Pumps & paths > 2 Syringes > 3 Timeline editor > 4 Check & simulate.

Manual: connect real pumps or the mock controller, select the pumps used, and confirm physical addresses/inlet connections. Enter the actual syringe inside diameters in mm. In step 3 check clamping, tubing and outlet/valve routing; confirm preparation or explicitly skip it. Optional finite priming has separate write, start and stop actions. Configure formal delivery, review the target pumps, write/read back settings, then explicitly start the listed pumps.

Immediate priming runs in manual step 3. Optional startup Prime runs when the reviewed program starts. Review both quantities. Every DIA/program rewrite invalidates previous program proof before dispatch, including failed writes. A diameter recheck alone cannot rearm old priming settings.

Timeline: plan offline, choose pumps/paths, enter diameters, and author blocks. Include any initial preparation directly as blocks. The four-column review counts every included block in the total delivery. Check the flow, then play the simulation. Device writes and independent priming are restricted to Manual Control.

Unapplied block and duration drafts survive mode switches and shared preparation updates. Single-pump review guidance follows the listed targets. Timeline editor and review playback share locks while pumps run, await STOP, or commands are in progress.

Stop all remains in the top bar, cancels simulation and sends STOP to all configured addresses. Confirm each pump response. Warning windows leave the main window accessible.

Shared syringe plans, selected tracks, fluid/inlet names and timeline blocks are saved in JSON. Manual delivery values and user/device confirmations belong to the current session. Imported files require a new preparation check; device proof is never imported.

Windows x64 and Windows 11 ARM64 share the installer. See BILINGUAL_MANUAL_UI_v1.4.1.md and UI_REVIEW_v1.4.1.md for details. Timeline playback is simulation only. Actual delivery, tubing behavior and pump synchronization need hardware measurements.
