NE-501 Pump Controller UI Preview v1.3.1

Single-pump workflow
1. Select the COM port and click Connect and detect. Detection runs automatically after connection. Rescan pumps checks the connections again.
2. Click Edit on a pump card. The settings heading and fixed action footer identify the pump and its actual address.
3. Enter the actual syringe inside diameter and click Verify diameter. A verified diameter collapses to one line; use Change to edit it. A diameter change invalidates verification and clears that pump's group selection with a visible explanation.
4. Set direction, main rate and unit, and Continuous or Target volume. The target volume applies to the main delivery phase.
5. Enable Prime before delivery if needed. Choose one of the five levels and a Prime volume. Prime uses the selected direction and switches to the main rate afterward.
6. Click 3 Apply and verify in the fixed settings footer. Incomplete active fields show an inline explanation. Start stays disabled until device verification succeeds.
7. Click 4 Start pump in the same footer. Stop this pump is always in that footer; each card also retains its Start/Stop shortcuts. Stop all pumps stays at the top right. Check the device response to confirm stopping.

Group controls
Group becomes available after each pump's diameter is verified. Editing a pump and selecting it for group actions are separate choices. The group summary shows selection, readiness and addresses. A blocker identifies the pump and the required action. Resolve navigates to the relevant settings or status control without sending device commands.
Apply selected, Start selected and Stop selected show their target count. All selected pumps must be verified before group start. Each pump uses its own settings. Existing queued-command cancellation and stop confirmation remain in use.

Display and status
The four-step strip follows Diameter, Delivery, Verify and Start. Yellow outlines mark the current required action. The right settings area starts at about 500 px and the divider is draggable. Compact cards keep individual Stop controls visible at smaller window heights. Hover a compact card for its delivery mode and last device-report time.
Last checked time is information; an unknown device state, alarm, changed diameter or unapplied setting has its own instruction. Auto refresh is optional and starts off. Busy communications skip its 2-second trigger.
Language changes preserve values, drafts, connection and selection, and send no pump commands. Diagnostic TX/RX and CSV records retain device data.

Manual control sends commands to connected pumps. Timeline playback remains simulation only. Real pump flow, timing and synchronization require hardware validation. See DISCLAIMER.txt and BILINGUAL_MANUAL_UI_v1.3.1.md.

v1.3.1 timeline syringe setup
Use Syringes to configure the actual inside diameters for all used pumps. Manual verified read-backs can be copied explicitly into the plan. The inspector shows planned/device diameter, plunger speed and the representable device rate. Changes invalidate the flow check and are saved with the workflow. Legacy workflows require the missing diameters to be supplied; the example explicitly uses 10 mm example diameters.
Flow is volumetric. Plunger speed equals flow in microlitres/min divided by syringe area in square millimetres. Preflight checks numeric representability and the manufacturer's mechanical range. Timeline playback remains simulation; the planning dialog sends no device commands.
The update fixes four-digit integer zero truncation, loose read-back tolerances, diameter precision, same-file reload after Save, extreme numeric input, CSV filename collisions and invalid-address truncation. See SOURCE_AUDIT_v1.3.1.md.
